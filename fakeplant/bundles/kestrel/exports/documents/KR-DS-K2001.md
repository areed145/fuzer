# Ariel JGK/4 reciprocating compressor datasheet

## Rating

Four-throw, two-stage separable reciprocating compressor, Caterpillar gas engine driver. Rated 52972 Mscf/d at 348 psig suction and 1015 psig discharge, 1200 rpm.

## Cylinders

| Throw | Stage | Bore (mm) | Rod (mm) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 241 | 63.5 |
| 2 | 1 | 241 | 63.5 |
| 3 | 2 | 178 | 63.5 |
| 4 | 2 | 178 | 63.5 |

Double-acting cylinders. Each cylinder has its own discharge temperature transmitter (B1-TT-2021 to B1-TT-2024); interstage pressure is B1-PT-2007.

## Limits

Maximum discharge temperature 302 °F. Frame vibration alarm 0.280 in/s, trip 0.441 in/s. Frame gas rod load rating 95 kN per throw, in compression and in tension; the rod load must reverse every revolution.

## Driver and lube oil

Caterpillar G3608 gas engine, eight cylinders, each with its own exhaust temperature transmitter (B1-TT-2031 to B1-TT-2038). Frame lube oil pressure B1-PT-2008 from the shaft-driven pump: low alarm 41 psig, trip 29 psig; the electric prelube pump must build pressure before a start.

## Spares

Valve assembly JGK-VA-4, piston ring set JGK-PR-8, rod packing set JGK-RP-2.
