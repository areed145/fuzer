# Kimray PV-45 glycol pump datasheet

## Rating

Gas-energy-exchange glycol circulation pump, 45 gallons per hour maximum, for triethylene glycol dehydration.

## Maintenance

Packing kit PV45-PK every quarter or on visible leakage; check valves PV45-CV at each service. A leaking pump circulates less glycol and the contactor's dry gas water content rises.
