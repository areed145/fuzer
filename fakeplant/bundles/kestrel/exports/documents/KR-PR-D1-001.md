# KRGP train D1: amine unit restart and foaming response

## Scope

Used when gas comes back through the amine contactor D1-V-4001 after a compressor trip or a plant shutdown, and whenever the contactor differential pressure D1-PDT-4001 rises.

## Restart

1. Keep lean amine circulating through the trip (D1-P-4008A on duty, D1-FT-4009 about 18 m3/h) so the regenerator D1-V-4005 stays hot.
2. Bring gas back slowly: a step change in gas rate is the usual trigger for foaming.
3. Watch D1-PDT-4001 and the sweet gas CO2 analyser D1-AT-4002 for the first two hours.

## Foaming

Contactor dP above 0.6 bar, or rising sweet gas CO2, is foaming until shown otherwise. Inject antifoam at the lean amine pump suction, cut gas rate if D1-AT-4002 approaches the 2.0 mol% sales limit, and check the amine carbon filter D1-F-4010: a spent carbon element (ACF40-CE) lets hydrocarbons and degradation products build up and the amine foams again at the next upset.

## Reboiler

Hold the amine reboiler D1-E-4006 at 118 to 124 degC with hot oil from G1-H-7001. Above 127 degC the MDEA degrades and the reboiler corrodes.
