# KRGP train C1: TEG reboiler temperature and lean glycol purity

## Scope

Used when the dry gas water content at C1-AT-3011 rises toward the 112 mg/Sm3 sales limit, or the TEG reboiler C1-H-3008 will not hold temperature.

## Checks

1. Compare C1-AT-3011 with the latest lab water content at the C1-V-3001 outlet; the analyser and the lab should agree within 10 mg/Sm3.
2. Read C1-TT-3008. The reboiler runs at 200 to 204 degC; below 195 degC the lean glycol purity falls under 98.5 wt% and the contactor cannot dry the gas. Never exceed 204 degC: TEG degrades.
3. Check lean glycol circulation from C1-P-3002A or C1-P-3002B (procedure KR-PR-C1-001). A leaking pump and a cool reboiler together are the usual cause of wet sales gas.
4. If the burner is at full fire and C1-TT-3008 is still low, look at the flame: a lazy, yellow flame means a fouled burner nozzle or fire tube. Raise a notification for burner nozzle RB750-BN and a fire tube inspection.

## While the gas is wet

Wet gas forms hydrates in the gas/gas exchanger E1-E-5001 and the propane chiller E1-E-5002. Raise methanol from G1-P-7008A to E1-E-5001 until C1-AT-3011 is back under 112 mg/Sm3, and watch E1-TT-5003 for a rising chiller approach.
