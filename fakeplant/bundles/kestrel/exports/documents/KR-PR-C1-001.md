# KRGP train C1: glycol circulation pump changeover

## Scope

Changing duty between glycol circulation pumps C1-P-3002A and C1-P-3002B on the glycol contactor C1-V-3001.

## Steps

1. Check the standby pump's packing and suction strainer.
2. Open the standby pump's gas supply slowly; confirm it strokes and builds discharge pressure.
3. Close the duty pump's gas supply and confirm lean glycol flow to C1-V-3001 is held.
4. Record the changeover in the operator log.

## Troubleshooting

A packing leak on the duty pump reduces circulation and raises the sales gas water content: change over and repair the packing (kit PV45-PK). A pump that fails to start on changeover usually has a stuck start interlock.
