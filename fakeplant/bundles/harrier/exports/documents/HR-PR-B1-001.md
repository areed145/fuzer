# HRPT area B1: running the midline station on one pump

With one midline pump out of service, the line runs at up to 113217 bbl/d on the other. Keep B1-PT-2003 suction above 87 psig by holding the origin discharge A1-PT-1005 at 1233 psig or more. With no standby at the midline, a trip of the running pump stops the line: the pipeline controller cuts origin rate to 69188 bbl/d until it restarts.
