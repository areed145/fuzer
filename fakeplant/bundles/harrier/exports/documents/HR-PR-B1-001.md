# HRPT area B1: running the midline station on one pump

With one midline pump out of service, the line runs at up to 18000 sm3/d on the other. Keep B1-PT-2003 suction above 6 barg by holding the origin discharge A1-PT-1005 at 85 barg or more. With no standby at the midline, a trip of the running pump stops the line: the pipeline controller cuts origin rate to 11000 sm3/d until it restarts.
